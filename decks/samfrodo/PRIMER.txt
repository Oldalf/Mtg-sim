This deck is an Abzan tokens and aristocrats deck with Sam and Frodo as commanders.

It makes tokens, Treasures and Food, sacrifices them, and every token that appears or dies drains the table. Life gain feeds a second drain plan through Gollum and Sanguine Bond. I usually attack with only Frodo (for the Ring and the cards) and Gollum (for his trigger).

HOW IT WORKS
Sam makes a Food at the beginning of combat and makes Foods cheaper to eat, so every turn feeds the engine. Peregrin Took and Tippy-Toe add a Food to every token event, and Academy Manufactor turns each Food, Clue or Treasure into one of each. Sacrifice outlets and death triggers turn that pile into drain.

WIN LINES
- Mirkwood Bats: each opponent loses 1 whenever you create a token or sacrifice one.
- Marionette Apprentice: each opponent loses 1 whenever a creature or artifact you control dies. Marionette Master: the drain is its power for every artifact that dies.
- Popular Egotist: a ping and 1 life for every permanent you sacrifice. Susurian Voidborn does the same when creatures and artifacts die.
- Sanguine Bond and Gollum: life gain hurts the table. Eat Foods and Gollum's end-step trigger hits every opponent he connected with. Sanguine Bond targets one opponent, so spread the drain.
- Frodo: gain 3 life, then attack and the Ring tempts you. Frodo draws once tempted twice, and at level 4 his hit drains 3 from each opponent.
- Rapacious Guest drains its power when it leaves the battlefield. Own removal on it (Swords, Path to Exile, Go for the Throat) is a real line once it is big.
- Merry and Pippin find each other when cast. Pippin gives the others +3/+3 and haste, for an alpha strike when it is lethal.

MULLIGANS
The first mulligan is free in my pod (a new 7), then it is a London mulligan (draw 7, bottom the extras). From simulating thousands of opening hands:
- Keep 3 or 4 lands. Keep 2, 5 or 6 lands when the hand has a ramp piece, a token or Food maker, or a drain payoff. Mulligan 0 or 1 lands (a one-lander is nearly a full turn slower than a new seven) and 7 lands. After the free mulligan, 2 to 6 lands is fine.
- Look for sources of all three colours (white, black and green). That is worth about a third of a turn, so lands that fix (Command Tower, Path of Ancestry, Exotic Orchard, the snarls) are worth keeping.
- The best cards to see early are Academy Manufactor, Peregrin Took, Mirkwood Bats, Nuka-Cola Vending Machine, The Notary Hobbits, Sol Ring, Gollum and Gilded Goose. A hand with one of the first seven wins about a turn sooner, so it is an easy keep.
- A token or Food maker and a drain payoff are each worth about a quarter of a turn. Ramp pieces are worth a little less.
- Shire Shirriff, Unlucky Cabbage Merchant, Farmer Cotton (better with a big X later), Mentor of the Meek and Gwaihir don't help early. Don't keep a hand for them.
- Removal looks weak in a simulation only because the opponents there do nothing. In a real game a hand with removal is a fine keep.
