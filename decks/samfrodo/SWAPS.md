# Sam & Frodo: swaps

## Made on 2026-11-24 (cards bought; now in `decklist.txt`)

| In | Out | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Agent of the Iron Throne | Farmer Cotton | each Food, Treasure or Clue sacrificed with Sam and Frodo out drains 2 from each opponent; on its own +5.3 points by turn 10 in the list B test (`PLAN.md`) |
| Llanowar Wastes | Access Tunnel | a coloured source instead of a colourless utility land |
| Caves of Koilos | Graypelt Refuge | untapped instead of always tapped |
| Eternal Witness | Pristine Talisman | the card comes from Meren (cut there on 2026-11-24); gets removal back in a real game |

**Result** (16,000 goldfish games, same seeds, noise about ±2 points as printed; old = `decklist.txt` before the swaps):

| Measure | Old | New | New + Eternal Witness for Pristine Talisman |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win by turn 10 | 67.9% | 74.4% (+6.5) | 73.8% (+5.9) |
| Win by turn 12 | 95.5% | 97.1% (+1.6) | 97.0% (+1.5) |
| Win by turn 14 | 99.3% | 99.5% | 99.5% |
| Turn the slowest 10% win by | 12 | 11 | 11 |
| Slow start (under 9 mana by turn 5 or under 13 by turn 6) | 8.3% | 7.2% | 7.5% |
| Average mana spent by turn 5 / 6 | 13.6 / 20.3 | 13.8 / 20.5 | 13.7 / 20.3 |
| Thin board at end of turn 5 | 2.0% | 1.7% | 1.8% |

The swaps are a clear gain (the right column is the deck as it is now). Eternal Witness for Pristine Talisman changes nothing in goldfish (−0.6, within noise): its value is getting removal back, which only a pod shows (list B in the pod: about +1 to +1.5 points of win rate, at the edge of the noise).
