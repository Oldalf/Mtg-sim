# Fire Lord Azula (Friend 3's deck): deck plan

Grixis spells, bracket 4. A friend's deck, **pod-only** (Pontus, 2026-11-15). Run it with `npm run pod -- azula ...`.

## Files

- `cards.ts`: names, `VALUE`, `HOLD`, `FINISHERS`, `X_SPELLS`. `game.ts`: `AzulaGame` (extends `CreatureDeckGame`): the cast triggers on every spell (Azula, Vivi, Storm of Saruman, Prismari, Arcane Bombardment), the free casts of copies (Arcane Bombardment, Mysterious Stranger, Torrential Gearhulk), Ozai, Sozin's Comet, the flash permissions, the equipment, Fated Firepower. Every other card's rules are in the engine (`engine/card-rules/`; U1b step 2, 2026-11-26). `spells.ts`: what the deck's other spells do (Scarring Memories, Sozin's Comet, Torment of Hailfire). Seething Song, Mana Geyser and Jeska's Will are engine card rules (U1b step 3, 2026-11-26). `combos.ts`: the ten combos. `candidates.ts`. `tests/azula.test.ts`.

## How the deck wins (Pontus, 2026-11-16)

Value engines, spells cast in combat while Azula attacks (she copies each one, her firebending pays for the first), free casts, and many infinite combos. The ten combos (Commander Spellbook, "Agni Kai") are listed in "The ten combos" below. All ten count, with two conditions Pontus set:

- 4, 5, 8, 9 and 10 only make endless magecraft triggers: they count only with **Storm-Kiln Artist** out (its Treasures make the mana endless).
- A combo needs a **finisher it can reach**: Vivi Ornitier when the loop casts a spell each round, or endless mana and a finisher spell (Lightning Bolt, Kill! Maim! Burn!, Breath of Malfegor, Electrodominance, Torment of Hailfire, Scarring Memories) in hand, or in the library when the loop digs (Frantic Search loots, Jeska's Will with Azula out exiles, the Archmages draw). With red mana only (Jeska's Will, Mana Geyser), the finisher's other pips must be payable.

## Simulation rules

- Win: every opponent at 0 life. Second measure: first opponent dead. Combat: the engine's.
- **A combo** is found by `findComboWin` (each check: the pieces, the timing, the mana for the first round). It is not played round by round: the first round is paid, the log says what the loop makes and the finisher, and every opponent dies. In a pod it is a declared win (`winAttempt`): the others may counter one of its spells (or the finisher) or remove a piece (Storm-Kiln Artist, Prismari, Twinning Staff, Azula, Vivi); then the opponents' life goes back and the turn goes on.
- Jeska's Will counts the fullest opponent's hand; Mana Geyser the opponents' tapped lands (a pod: the real ones through the Arena; goldfish: `opp_hand` 7 and `opp_tapped_per_turn` 2.15 a turn, as Codie). Six cards or lands break even: no endless mana then (Vivi or Storm-Kiln Artist still win).
- **Azula attacks** (in a pod she must attack while we hold something to cast in combat). Her firebending 2 (the engine's) is in the pool, and the AI casts in combat what it held back in the first main phase: instants, flash cards, and with Leyline of Anticipation, High Fae Trickster, Valley Floodcaller or Wizards of Thay the rest too. Each spell is copied (a permanent's copy is a token, not a legendary one). A combo comes first. Sozin's Comet gives each attacker firebending 5; Firebender Ascension's Soldier has firebending 1 and the Ascension copies firebending from its fourth quest counter.
- **Copies and free casts:** Storm of Saruman (the second spell each turn; its copy of a legendary is kept), Prismari (storm), Twinning Staff (one more copy), Arcane Bombardment (the first instant or sorcery each turn: a random one from the graveyard is exiled, then each exiled card is cast again free), Mysterious Stranger (a pod: a random instant or sorcery from our graveyard cast free), Torrential Gearhulk (the best instant in our graveyard, cast free, then exiled). Free casts trigger what casting does (Azula copies them).
- **Engines:** Storm-Kiln Artist, Archmage Emeritus, Archmage of Runes, Veyran (the engine's `spell-copies.ts`), Vivi Ornitier (a noncreature spell: a +1/+1 counter and 1 damage to each opponent), Mindsplice Apparatus (an oil counter each upkeep, each one makes instants and sorceries cost {1} less), Wizards of Thay, Fated Firepower (damage to opponents +X), Ozai (mana we would lose becomes red), Rhystic Study and Phyrexian Arena (engine).
- **Equipment:** Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots and Whispersilk Cloak go on Azula (haste; shroud or hexproof against the pod's removal; the Cloak makes her unblockable).
- **Spells:** burn is cast in combat while Azula attacks (copied), when it kills, or late in a second main phase without Azula; draw spells (Big Score, Unexpected Windfall, Demand Answers, Frantic Search, Sea Gate Restoration, Jeska's Will with Azula out) for value; Waterlogged Teachings fetches the one card a combo still lacks. Thought Vessel, Decanter and Sea Gate Restoration: no maximum hand size.
- **Interaction** is held in a goldfish game; in a pod it answers (engine `answers.ts`): Counterspell, Mana Drain, Void Shatter, Force of Will (free by pitching a blue card and 1 life when we can't pay it), Fierce Guardianship (free with Azula out), Sink into Stupor (counted as a counter), Deadly Rollick, Kill! Maim! Burn!, Isildur's Fateful Strike, Untimely Malfunction, Snap; Necron Deathmark destroys a creature on entering.

## Not modelled

Reiterate, Reverberate and Narset's Reversal outside the combos (copying a burn or draw spell for value), Seething Song and Mana Geyser as plain rituals, Torment of Hailfire outside a combo, Kill! Maim! Burn!'s burn outside a combo, Electrodominance's free spell, Vivi's mana ability, Mirror Room // Fractured Realm (held), Callidus Assassin's copy (a plain body), Faerie Mastermind's draws, Deflecting Swat, Redirect Lightning, Vandalblast, Fire Nation Palace's firebending, Arena of Glory's exert, the life payment of Sink into Stupor and Sea Gate Restoration's lands, Leyline of Anticipation from the opening hand, Scarring Memories' sacrifice and discard, Ozai's flying and indestructible, Necron Deathmark's mill, Mysterious Stranger in a goldfish game (no other graveyards), Isildur's Fateful Strike needing a legendary creature.

## First numbers (2026-11-16)

- Goldfish, 2,000 games: win by turn 8 / 10 / 12 / 14: 8.3 / 20.6 / 40.8 / 63.5%, average turn 11.27 (37% no win by turn 14). First opponent dead by turn 10: 69%. A combo goes off in about 29% of games (500 seeds): mostly Mana Geyser and Jeska's Will with Reiterate (goldfish opponents hold 7 cards) and combo 7 (Azula + Narset's Reversal + Storm-Kiln Artist); the rest win by Azula's combat and burn.
- Pod against three goldfish decks (1,000 games): 84.6% wins, round 12.53.
- Pod with Meren, Edgar and Helga (1,000 games): Azula 5.7%, Meren 38.0%, Edgar 32.0%, Helga 24.2% (noise about 3 points). Azula is out first in 48% of games, around round 8: the creature decks kill her before her combos come together, and the real opponents seldom hold the 6+ cards Jeska's Will needs. Only 0.04 win attempts a game. `azula_attacks_to_cast=false` changed nothing (5.0%).
- **Likely underrated:** the AI only goes off when every piece and the whole first round's mana are there at once; it does not use Reiterate, Seething Song or Mana Geyser as plain ramp to reach a combo turn earlier, does not hold counterspells to protect a combo, and Vivi's mana is not modelled. A good pilot of a bracket 4 list would be faster.

## The ten combos (Pontus's answers, 2026-11-16, moved here from the root PLAN.md, task B6)

Bracket 4. The combos (Commander Spellbook, "Agni Kai"); all ten count as a win:
1. Reiterate + Jeska's Will: an opponent has 6+ cards in hand. Infinite red.
2. Reiterate + Mana Geyser: the opponents have 6+ tapped lands together. Infinite red.
3. Storm-Kiln Artist + Seething Song + Reiterate: infinite Treasures.
4. Twinning Staff + Narset's Reversal (an instant or sorcery to cast first, plus {7} for the Staff).
5. Narset's Reversal + Reverberate (another instant or sorcery in hand).
6. Fire Lord Azula attacking + Narset's Reversal + Frantic Search (up to three lands that make {U}{U}{U}): infinite looting and land mana.
7. Fire Lord Azula attacking + Narset's Reversal + Storm-Kiln Artist (another instant or sorcery castable with at most four coloured mana): infinite Treasures.
8. Narset's Reversal + Reiterate (another instant or sorcery in hand).
9. Prismari, the Inspiration + Reiterate (another instant or sorcery).
10. Prismari, the Inspiration + Reverberate (another instant or sorcery).

4, 5, 8, 9 and 10 only make infinite magecraft triggers, so they count **only with Storm-Kiln Artist on the battlefield** (it triggers on copies too: infinite Treasures). Archmage Emeritus (in this list) would also draw the library with them. A combo needs a **finisher it can reach** (Pontus picked this over a declared win once the pieces are there): a burn spell or Torment of Hailfire in hand to copy or pay X for, Vivi Ornitier out (it pings on each cast), or a draw spell to copy until a finisher turns up. Without one it is just a big turn. Sim runs in eco.

**What Pontus said about how the deck really plays (2026-11-16):**
- Several combos also draw. Frantic Search + Narset's Reversal lets the deck draw as much as it wants (draw 2, discard 2, again and again) while netting one mana a round, "if there are at least 6 lands in play to untap". So Azula can search freely for a combo win.

Or a Torment of Hailfire at instant speed for a huge X.

**What the code does now** (all in `decks/azula/`):
- `combos.ts`: `findComboWin(game)` tries the ten checks (`jeskasWillLoop` ... `prismariReverberateLoop`); each returns a `ComboPlan` (the spells, the first round's `cost`, the permanents it needs, a `LoopOutput`: endless red / any mana, casts or copies each round, digs the library) or null. `reachableFinisher` applies Pontus's finisher rule (Vivi Ornitier when the loop casts each round; endless mana and a `FINISHERS` card in hand, or in the library when the loop digs; red-only mana needs the finisher's other pips payable). `goOffWithCombo` pays the first round, logs `COMBO: ...` and calls `game.killEveryOpponent(win)` (a declared win; `winAttempt` lists the spells and permanents a pod opponent may answer). `missingComboPiece` names the card a tutor should fetch.
- `candidates.ts`: a ready combo is a candidate at score 1000 (checked every step of `develop`). In the first main phase, when Azula can attack, cards castable in combat wait (`canCastInCombat`); in combat (`onAttack` in `game.ts` calls `developWin`) they are cast and Azula copies each one.
- `cards.ts` `HOLD`: never cast on their own: the counterspells and removal (the pod casts them as answers), **Reiterate, Reverberate, Narset's Reversal, Seething Song, Mana Geyser, Torment of Hailfire, Kill! Maim! Burn!** (only a combo uses them), Mirror Room.
- The engine (`engine/game/game-core.ts`): `develop()` picks candidates by score, with the planner (`plan`, and `rolloutValue` in `engine/game/planner.ts`: each option tried on a copy, the rest of the turn and the next turn played greedily, scored by `positionValue`) choosing among the top `planner_width` (4) affordable ones. A combo found in a rollout scores 9000+, so a setup play the planner sees leads to a combo is picked, but only if it is a candidate at all: the HOLD cards never are.

**The diagnosis to confirm first (part 1), and the likely causes:**
1. **No setup turns.** A combo goes off only when every piece and the whole first round's mana are there at once. Seething Song and Mana Geyser (held) are never cast as ramp to reach a combo; Big Score and Unexpected Windfall's Treasures are not saved for one; the AI does not hold a combo piece back from being discarded on purpose (`discardValue` comes from `VALUE`: check that every combo piece and finisher is valued above the lands and the extra cards).
2. **No digging toward a missing piece.** With all but one piece, the draw spells and Waterlogged Teachings are not pushed (Teachings is, at score 75, only for instants: `missingComboPiece(game, (other) => other.isInstant)`).
3. **Frantic Search + Narset's Reversal is only a combo while Azula attacks** (combo 6). Pontus's point is that it digs freely. Check the loop by the rules, step by step, with and without Azula attacking, and write the arithmetic here: what each round costs ({2}{U} + {U}{U}, Archmage of Runes / Wizards of Thay / Mindsplice make Frantic cheaper), what it untaps (3 lands per Frantic Search copy that resolves), how many copies resolve per round (Azula's copy, the Narset copy, Twinning Staff, Veyran does not add copies), and so how many lands it needs to gain mana each round (Spellbook says three lands that make {U}{U}{U}; Pontus says six lands). Without Azula: Narset's Reversal copies Frantic Search and returns the original, so the original never resolves; with cost reducers out it may still net mana. Model what the rules give, with a test for each case.
4. **Azula dies early in a pod** (out first 48%, round 8). Find out how: combat (blocked or attacked), removal, or the player's life. Count in a pod run (part 1). The threat the others see is `podClose()` in `game.ts` (0.3 for Azula on the battlefield, 0.3 Storm-Kiln Artist, ...) times `threat_close` (25) in `Game.threat()` (`engine/game/game.ts`); the attack target is chosen by `attackPreference` (`engine/pod/pod-interaction.ts`), which uses the threat. If Azula is focused because `podClose` is too high next to the other decks', compare with how Aesi and Gale set theirs and say so; do not tune it to make the numbers look good.
5. **Nothing happens on another player's turn.** The instant-speed combos (3, 4, 5, 8 are all instants) could go off at an opponent's end step, after the others tapped out. The pod gives a deck no turn of its own outside its turn except answers: `engine/pod/pod.ts` (about line 95) calls `opponentUpkeep()` for the other players and nothing at the end step. A hook such as `opponentEndStep()` called for each other living player after the active player's `takeTurn()` would let Azula try `findComboWin` there (with its untapped mana). That is a pod engine change: save a fixed-seed pod run before (`npm run pod -- gale meren samfrodo aesi edgar -n 200 --no-log`, scratchpad) and it must be IDENTICAL after (no other deck uses the hook), and `npm run pod -- --test`.
