This deck is a Simic landfall deck, built for bracket 3.

The goal is a deck that is consistently fun to play and not awful to play against. It is a modified version of the Reverse Landfall deck by DefCatMtg: his list was the starting point, with cards I own instead of a few expensive ones.

HOW IT WORKS
Aesi draws a card for every land that enters. The moonfolk return a land you control to your hand, so you can play it again for another landfall trigger. Extra land drops (Azusa, Loot, Case of the Locked Hothouse) and creatures that put a land from your hand onto the battlefield (Walking Atlas, Sakura-Tribe Scout, PuPu UFO, Budoka Gardener) turn that into a stream of cards. Biomancer's Familiar, Training Grounds and Heartstone make the moonfolk abilities cost as little as one mana, never less. I track land drops and lands put onto the battlefield separately with two dice.

LOOPS
Demonstrate a loop two or three times and then name the result. Stop before the library is empty. A loop needs a way to replay a land and a way to return it.
- Retreat to Coralhelm or Elven Raft-Steerer + a land-putting creature + Simic Growth Chamber: infinite landfall, so Aesi draws what you need.
- The same with a moonfolk ability at one mana: Meloku makes infinite Illusions, Soratami Mindsweeper mills everyone, Oboro Breezecaller untaps a land and pays for itself, Soratami Mirror-Mage returns three lands and gains mana with Biomancer's Familiar.
- Kodama of the East Tree + Meloku: infinite 1/1 flying Illusions.
- Uyo + Retreat + a putter, with two lands that make two mana: infinite copies of an instant or sorcery.
- Omniscience + Kiri-Onna + Haru-Onna: cast them for free over and over. Count the opponents' creatures, bounce them all and draw that many cards, then draw until you have what you need.
Lotus Cobra turns any of these into infinite mana.

WIN LINES
- Mill: the Mindsweeper loop, or a huge Blue Sun's Zenith copied by Uyo for every opponent.
- An army: Meloku's Illusions, or Dokai's big Elementals, attacking the next turn.
- Endless Swarm or Eternal Dominion (epic: you can't cast spells afterwards, and you copy it every upkeep). Uyo can make the copies much faster: about 10 to 20 Eternal Dominion copies take enough of the opponents' best cards to win by the next upkeep.

MULLIGANS
The first mulligan is free in my pod (a new 7), then it is a London mulligan (draw 7, bottom the extras). From simulating thousands of opening hands with this list:
- Keep 3 to 6 lands if one of them makes green. Lands are cards with Aesi, so a five-lander is the best hand.
- 2 lands is a keep only with a green source and a cheap ramp piece (Rampant Growth, Search for Tomorrow, Sakura-Tribe Elder, Lotus Cobra, Sol Ring). Otherwise it is slightly worse than a new seven.
- Mulligan 0 or 1 lands. A one-lander is worse than a six-card hand. Mulligan 7 lands too.
- A green source is the most important single thing, worth about a full turn: nearly all the ramp costs G. Forests, duals, fetch lands, Simic Growth Chamber and Command Tower all count. No green source: mulligan. Blue matters little early.
- The best cards to see early are Sol Ring, Lotus Cobra, Walking Atlas, Cultivate, Sakura-Tribe Scout, PuPu UFO, Skyshroud Claim and Kodama. Any ramp piece that costs one or two is worth about half a turn.
- Kiri-Onna, Patron of the Moon, Soratami Mirror-Mage, Soratami Mindsweeper, Graceful Adept, Oboro Envoy, Soratami Mirror-Guard and Kira win games later but do nothing before turn 5. A hand of lands and one of them is fine; a hand with several of them and little mana is not.
- Aim to cast Aesi on turn 5 or 6. Keep hands that ramp toward six mana.
